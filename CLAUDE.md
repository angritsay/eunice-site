# Working on the Eunice site

Plain HTML generated by `apps/web/build.ts` (TypeScript, run directly by Node) from `apps/web/src/`. The pages we ship keep **zero runtime JavaScript dependencies** — no framework, no bundle, only our own `site.js`. Tooling and services may use packages under the supply-chain controls in [ADR-0002](docs/adr/0002-dependencies-under-supply-chain-controls.md): exact pins, a committed lockfile, a release quarantine, and a reason in the PR that adds one.

## How to make a change

1. Content that appears on more than one page (people, insights, events, quotes, roles, partners) lives only in `apps/web/src/content/index.ts`. Never paste it into a page.
2. Page copy lives in `apps/web/src/pages/<page>.ts`. Shared blocks live in `apps/web/src/components.ts`; reuse them before writing new markup.
3. Markup is written only with the `html` tag from `apps/web/src/lib/html.ts`: everything interpolated into it is escaped unless it is itself markup. `raw()` is for our own artwork (SVG paths), never for content. Content is checked against `apps/web/src/content/schema.ts` when the site builds; a new kind of content gets a schema there.
4. Run `pnpm build` and `pnpm test:web`, and make sure both pass. If you changed rendered HTML on purpose, run `pnpm test:web:update` and check the golden diff reads the way you meant. Add a new page to `apps/web/test/web/urls.json` in the same change.
5. Work on a new branch (`claude/<topic>`, or `yi/<topic>` when Yi is driving) and open a draft pull request with a one-line summary per change. Never push to `main`, never force-push, and never merge a pull request unless the repo owner asked for that merge in the session. `main` is protected: a pull request needs green checks and the owner's approval. Each open pull request is published at `https://angritsay.github.io/eunice-site/preview/pr-<N>/` a few minutes after each push (`.github/workflows/pages.yml`); give the user that link. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
6. Fix a thing that shows on several pages in the shared component (`components.ts`, `site.css`), so every page gets it. Never patch one page.
7. A significant decision gets a record in `docs/adr/` in the same pull request.

## Design rules (agreed with the team, Sep 2026)

- White ground only. The one grey is `--band` (cool, very light). A coloured tint never sits on a grey band. The only dark sections are the home "About" band (`band--ink`) and the closing call-to-action.
- Two colours: the site is ink on white, with the one grey `--band`. The master brand is black. A desk keeps only a hint of its colour (Private Markets blue, Digital Assets orange `#FA8846`, Token Disclosure gold, the `-field` tokens): the dot or mark beside its name and its glyph on a tile. Desk text, numbers, bullets, steps panels, plates and tiles are black and white; `-ink`, `-text` and `-tint` resolve to ink and the band for that reason. Do not bring colour back into text or panels without asking.
- Four type roles: `.h1`, `.h2`, body (`.body`, `.lead`, `.small`), caption (`.caption`, `.label`). No new sizes, no sub-headings.
- The scale went down 20% in September 2026, to leave more air around the type. Spacing did not move, so the gaps read wider. This put body copy at 13.6px and `.small` at 12px, under the 15px floor the team agreed earlier that year — the floor was dropped deliberately, not overlooked. Do not take it lower without asking.
- Separate things with a hairline rule on top, not with boxes, borders or shadows. No bordered cards. The one shadow is the raised product plate below.
- No numbering (01, 02, 03) and no small uppercase captions or eyebrows anywhere: they read as a generated layout. A section is named by a real heading (`sectionHead()`), metadata is plain small text, and a sequence that matters is drawn as a `path()`, not numbered.
- Every fact card carries its line illustration (`art.ts`, required by `facts()`), so a row reads before its words; a new fact picks an existing drawing or adds one there.
- The quote a page gives the stage is a `pullQuote()`: large, on ink, the opening mark in the desk's colour. Rows of client quotes stay `quoteBlock()` cards.
- Product imagery is mid-fi and drawn, never a screenshot or screen recording: `apps/web/src/ui.ts` draws one Eunice window (glyph and breadcrumb, rail, view) with fictional funds and tokens, as `uiAsk`, `uiRead`, `uiMonitor`, `uiReport`, and the film `uiFilm` (ask and cite, find, watch, the closing line) that site.js steps through. Ink on white; the desk colour marks only the one thing that matters in each drawing. Every drawing runs on the same nine-second loop and stops under reduced motion. By the owner's choice the film has no pause button: it holds while the pointer rests on it, and stays still for visitors who ask for reduced motion. Add a new drawing to `ui.ts`, reusing the window and its views, so they stay recognisably one product.
- Proof is shown as numbers and badges (`numbers()`, `badges()` in `components.ts`): a big figure with a one-line label, and credentials named in outlined pills. Only figures already stated elsewhere on the site.
- Every drawing carries its own frame: the desk's background art (dunes for the company, misted ridges for Private Markets, copper for Crypto & RWA and Token Disclosure) shows only as a thin border round the window, 4% a side, about 15% of the drawing. The product is the rest. This holds for heroes (`heroField()`), feature plates, the films (`stage()`, the Home hero) and the Home tiles; change the frame in `site.css` (`.wf`, `.wf__win`), never per page. Background art is abstract, blurred nature generated to the palette; no city or street photography.
- A feature row (`feature()`) gives text and visual equal halves, so the rhythm holds when rows alternate sides.
- A blog block with one featured card puts the card on the left and the list beside it (`insightsBlock()`).
- Quotes (`quoteBlock()`): the same warm ground for every quote, the words in JetBrains Mono, the desk named by its mark in the desk colour.
- Space between blocks comes from `--section` / `--section-tight`: at least 96px on desktop, 80px below 1024px. `test/web/layout.spec.ts` fails the build under that.
- Navigation depends on the destination: company pages show the products; each product page shows its own audiences (`apps/web/src/site.config.ts`).
- Private Markets is about operational due diligence, portfolio monitoring and the end-to-end service. Regulation and the FCA sandbox belong to Digital Assets only. Trust markers on Private Markets: SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, no training on client data.
- The primary call to action is "Book demo".
- Long-form copy imported from the live site (job descriptions, blog posts) lives as HTML fragments in `apps/web/src/content/{jobs,posts}/`, written by `pnpm --filter @eunice/web import:live jobs|posts`. Never hand-write markup there: re-import instead. Fragments reach a page only through `richText()` (`src/lib/rich-text.ts`), which fails the build on any tag, attribute, link or image outside its allowlist.
- Exception: pages ported from the live eunice.ai site (`mica-whitepaper.ts`, `security.ts`) keep the live copy word for word, CTA labels included, and the live look (the `.mica-*` / `.sec-*` styles). Do not edit their copy or bring them under the rules above unless the owner asks.

## Copy

- Write like the desk talks to a client: plain, specific, no hype words ("revolutionary", "unlock", "game-changing").
- Judge a line by what it tells the reader, not by its length. Cut a line that carries nothing.
