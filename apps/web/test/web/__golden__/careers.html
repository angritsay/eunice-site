A Company Built on Accountability

Founded in 2023 · headquartered in London

Generative AI swept into financial workflows — and institutions struggled to use it for anything that mattered. The problem wasn’t capability. It was accountability.

In our world, an answer isn’t “good” because it sounds plausible — it’s good when it can be defended in front of a regulator, a board, or a client.

So we built Eunice: AI for monitoring in regulated finance, engineered for decisions that cannot afford to be wrong. Today serving institutions, funds, issuers, and regulators across the US, EU, UK and Singapore.

Named After Eunice Foote

Eunice Newton Foote, 1819–1888, scientist and campaigner for women’s rights. In 1856 she showed that carbon dioxide traps heat, the first person to connect it to the Earth’s temperature. Someone else took the credit; her result was overlooked until 2011.

We took her name because the finding sat in plain sight for a hundred and fifty years and nobody read it carefully. That is the failure this company exists to prevent.
