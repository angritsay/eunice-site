Client Implementation Consultant, Private Markets

Job Description at a Glance:

As a Client Implementation Consultant, you'll be part of a dynamic team at the forefront of transforming the financial landscape through AI. We offer a collaborative work culture that values creativity and accountability. With opportunities for professional growth and direct engagement with industry leaders, this role is perfect for those looking to make a significant impact in the private markets sector.

  • Tasks: Own client onboarding end to end and turn signed asset owners into live, confident users of Eunice's AI for private markets.
  • Company: Join a pioneering fintech startup revolutionising private markets with cutting-edge, AI-native technology.
  • Benefits: Competitive salary and a dynamic, high-trust work environment.
  • Collaborate with a small, research-heavy team in London, working either in person 2 days a week or fully remote
  • Why this job: Be at the forefront of a major shift in finance and make a real impact on how diligence gets done.
  • Qualifications: at least 2 years in private markets, with strong organisational and client-facing skills.

Location: Location: Either hybrid (2 days/week in office) OR fully remote

Employment Type: Part-time/ consulting basis OR Full-time

Department: Client / Commercial

Reports to: Petronela Pell, Head of Private Markets

What we are building:

The current major technological shift in finance saw AI analyzing the market. We are building the company that finishes the job. We're creating a truly vertical AI that finishes the last mile for alternative assets, starting with digital assets. We're bridging the gap between regulators and industry leaders on real-world workflows, and now we're expanding into the private markets.

It's a world with trillions in assets, waiting for transformation. In the coming years, every financial institution will have an AI agent that doesn't just analyze, but acts with accountability. If that vision excites you, come help us build it.

Your role

Your role is to make every new client successful from day one. As our asset owner clients come on board, you'll own the journey from signed contract to live, value-generating deployment. You'll run onboarding end to end, configure the platform to each client's workflows and data, and be the trusted point of contact who keeps implementations on track. You'll sit at the intersection of clients, product, and engineering, translating messy real-world requirements into a smooth go-live, and channeling what you learn back into a smarter product. As we scale, you'll build the implementation playbook that lets us onboard the next wave of clients with the same rigour.

What you'll do:

  • Lead client onboarding end to end from kickoff, implementation plan, timelines, and go-live.
  • Configure the platform to each client's workflows, data sources, and reporting needs, partnering with product and engineering where customisation is required.
  • Be the day-to-day point of contact during implementation, setting clear expectations and proactively flagging risks to timelines.
  • Run training and build onboarding materials that drive fast, confident adoption.
  • Capture client feedback and recurring requests, and feed them to the Product owners to shape the roadmap.
  • Build and refine our implementation playbook, templates, and processes as the business scales.

What we're looking for:

  • At least 2 years in private markets, financial services, or a related field e.g. at an asset owner, allocator, GP, fund administrator, consultancy, or fintech serving the asset class.
  • Familiarity with private markets workflows (fund structures, diligence, manager selection, reporting, or portfolio monitoring).
  • Strong project management and organisational skills; comfortable juggling multiple clients and deadlines.
  • Excellent communication and the ability to build trust with sophisticated institutional clients.
  • A collaborative, ownership-driven mindset and comfort working across clients, product, and engineering.
  • Comfort with software and data; eagerness to get hands-on with an AI platform.
  • Work on one of the biggest shifts happening in institutional investing.

We're a 12-person, tech- and research-heavy team, built for execution and depth. If you want to help reinvent how private markets clients experience an AI-native platform, and build a new category from the ground up, we'd love to hear from you.
