What Eunice does

Three things, in the order they happen.

  1. A view per asset The team, the code, the reserve and the jurisdiction in one place, so the reason for holding is written down.
  2. Changes as they land Protocol changes, enforcement actions and reserve movements surfaced the hour they happen, with the source named.
